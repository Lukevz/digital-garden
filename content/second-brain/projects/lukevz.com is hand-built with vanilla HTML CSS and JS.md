My site, lukevz.com, is vanilla HTML, CSS, and JS. No framework. The black hole particle background is canvas. It's a Bear-style note browser, which makes the site itself the public layer of [[A digital garden is a philosophy not a website|my digital garden]].

So yes, I write code. I hand-built this whole site myself, I'm building [[52 is about owning your thinking again|52]], and tinkering with code on side projects is how a lot of my evenings go, see [[My daily routine runs 9 to 5 from home]]. I'm a designer rather than an engineer by title, but building things is part of how I think.

It's organized into sections you can browse: writing at /#writing, videos at /#videos, photos at /#photos, and my career at /#career. What's in each one is mapped at [[Site MOC]]. Everything behind it is plain markdown in a repo, for the reason in [[File over app is why I keep everything in markdown]], which is also why the publishing pipeline in that note stopped mattering once I built this.

The chat on the site is powered by a free-tier LLM with a hand-written knowledge base of stuff about me. If it gets something wrong, DM me and I'll fix the source file; contact paths are at [[LinkedIn DM is the best way to reach me]]. Hub: [[PKM MOC]].
