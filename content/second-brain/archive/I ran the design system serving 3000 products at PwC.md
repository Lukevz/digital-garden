At PwC I owned the design system that serviced 3,000+ products globally. I led pattern and component work as manager, mentored a team of three designers, and ran the Figma migration that affected 120+ designers.

Everything I believe in [[Design systems are a shared language not a component library]] got stress-tested here: at that scale, adoption and shared language matter far more than component count.
