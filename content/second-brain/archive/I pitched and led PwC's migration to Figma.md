At PwC I pitched the switch from Sketch, Abstract, and InVision to Figma, rebuilt the design system to prove it out, and pioneered one of the two pilot projects. Then I taught the whole department: 120+ designers upskilled through documentation, office hours, and a self-made curriculum.

Figma has been my main design tool ever since; see [[Figma and Bear are my daily tools]]. The system I rebuilt is covered at [[I ran the design system serving 3000 products at PwC]]. Hubs: [[Design Systems MOC]], [[Career MOC]].
