It lines up with how I actually work and rest. [[My daily routine runs 9 to 5 from home]] and I like it that way, though [[Remote work is efficient but you still need people in a room]] is the honest caveat: I still think you need in-person time, I just don't need a lot of it. Socially I'm [[I am a texter not a caller]], and my version of a good weekend is [[A good Saturday is one thing done slowly]] rather than a packed calendar. Hub: [[Life MOC]].
