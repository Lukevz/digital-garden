The lesson running through my whole design systems career is that tokens are the unlock. They're what make multiple themes manageable, they're what we were missing for years before Figma shipped variables, and they're the layer that lets a small team control a big system, which is why [[Most companies do not need a custom design system]].

There's an AI angle now too. In current workflows, agents consume design through the context around it, and design tokens are that context. If you build on a popular base like Material, shadcn/ui, or Tailwind, you get to leverage all the public material and training those agents already have about it. Implement something fully bespoke and the agents know nothing. That's the same accelerator logic as [[AI amplifies your process it does not fix it]]: the leverage goes to whoever structures their system so the tools can use it.

Getting there the hard way is documented in [[Figma variables v1 caused more harm than help at scale]]. Hub: [[Design Systems MOC]].
