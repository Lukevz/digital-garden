Journaling finally stuck once I stopped trying to make it useful. Every version I'd tried before failed for the same reason, which I wrote about in [[Journaling is a habit I cannot make stick]]: I was trying to make the entries future-proof, aesthetic, or worth rereading. That turns it into a performance, and as a recovering people pleaser and perfectionist I'd rather do nothing than do it badly.

What I actually needed was a place to dump my thoughts. So in 2026 the habit is morning pages: every weekday, before I start work, I write down whatever's in my head. Thoughts, feelings, ideas, no structure, no audience, nothing to be proud of later. Some entries do turn into something worth revisiting, but that's a byproduct, not the point.

The part that made it work is where it sits in the day. In 2025 I'd wake up, make coffee, and go straight to Slack, see [[My daily routine runs 9 to 5 from home]]. Morning pages buy me a slow start and a sense of control before anyone else gets a say in my day.
