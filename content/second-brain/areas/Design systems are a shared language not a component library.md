A design system isn't a component library. It's a shared language, and the components are just the output. The real work is the decisions behind them, the documentation, and getting people to actually adopt it.

That's the thing people get wrong most often, and it's why design systems are my specialty. I've spent most of my career building and managing them, including [[I ran the design system serving 3000 products at PwC|the PwC design system]], and the pattern holds everywhere: a system succeeds or fails on adoption and shared understanding, not on how many components it ships. More on where this sits in my career at [[Design Systems MOC]].
