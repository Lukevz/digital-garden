My gym routine is always full body. I don't run a split. Every session is some strength training that hits the whole body rather than dedicating a day to one muscle group.

The other half is walking, and that's the part that does the heavy lifting. I work a desk job, so I'm always walking: on a walking treadmill during the day, or outside if the weather's good. That's the practical version of [[The basics cover most of my health]], where eating well, sleeping well, and moving enough covers maybe 70 percent of it including mental health, and it's the gym-and-walks half of [[My daily routine runs 9 to 5 from home]].
