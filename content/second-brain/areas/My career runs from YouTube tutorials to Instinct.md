I've been designing for about 12 to 13 years, depending on how you count. It started with YouTube tutorials and freelance work, went deep on UX and design systems at PwC, and landed at Instinct, where I design veterinary software today.

## The timeline

- 2012 to 2013: YouTube, self-started. Built a Mac OS app review and tutorial channel that grew to 30k+ subscribers and multi-million views. Taught myself writing, video, graphic design, and web from scratch.
- Around 2015 to 2018: Freelance. Solo client work in HTML/CSS, WordPress, graphic design, web design, and video production for local and regional businesses.
- 2018: Founded a creative production company, formalizing the freelance work into an official business.
- 2021: Blue Corona Inc, Senior Web Designer. Designed and built custom websites for 80+ home service companies. Fast-paced client work focused on marketing and delivery speed, and where [[I fell into design systems by drowning in website volume]].
- 2022: PwC Digital, Products & Technology, Senior UX Designer. Led proof-of-concept product designs, pitched and rebuilt the company's design system in Figma, and drove a department-wide migration from Sketch, Abstract, and InVision to Figma, upskilling 120+ designers.
- 2023: PwC Digital, UX Manager / Design System Manager. Led and mentored a team of three designers, managed the design system servicing 3,000+ products globally, and was lead designer on SDLC Canvas, the firm's flagship AI product.
- End of 2025: Joined Instinct Science as Senior Product Designer.
