I'm definitely a night owl. Something about my chronotype has always kept me from being a consistent morning person, and instead I get huge creative bursts of energy late at night, which then makes it tough to sleep. I'm rarely asleep before midnight.

Every few days, or a few days out of the month, I get a random urge to be a morning person. Maybe it's just that I can't sleep, but I wake up early, feel super refreshed, and get a ton done. It never sticks as a pattern though. This is the honest version of the 9 to 5 in [[My daily routine runs 9 to 5 from home]]. Hub: [[Life MOC]].
