My family is from South Africa. They moved to Atlanta in 1994 and I was the only one born here, since [[I am the youngest of four]]. I'm proud of that heritage.

I have three siblings: two brothers and a sister. Since I was the only one born in Atlanta, they were all born in South Africa before the move. I don't speak Afrikaans. My parents do a little bit, but it just wasn't something they taught us.

I still have family in South Africa. I haven't been able to visit in some time, but I've been a few times with family, and it's a gorgeous country. One of those trips is still one of the best of my life: [[South Africa gave me one of the best trips of my life]]. Where that leaves me day to day is [[Atlanta is home]]. Hub: [[Life MOC]].
