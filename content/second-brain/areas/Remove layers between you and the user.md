Every layer between a designer and the person they're solving for is a layer of removal. A PM, an engineer, leadership, whoever: those people can be great at their jobs and still dilute your understanding of the actual user.

Early in a project especially, you want as much direct signal as possible before adding other context in. This is a big part of why I moved to Instinct, the veterinary software company where I design now. Being close to real veterinarians beats reading a summary of them.
