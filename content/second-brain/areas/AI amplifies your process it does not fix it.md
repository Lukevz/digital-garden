AI is an accelerator. It amplifies your existing process, it can't fix a bad one. If your way of solving problems is already solid, you should see an exponential return on efficiency and impact. If it's not, AI just makes that worse faster.

That's why I care so much about the fundamentals: [[Define the baseline first]] and the [[Six questions before touching any tools]] matter more with AI in the loop, not less. The flip side of this take is [[AI makes taste the differentiator]]. How much AI actually changes the core of design work versus the peripheral stuff, I hold loosely. I think the instinct and taste are irreplaceable, but I'm watching that one. More at [[Design MOC]].
