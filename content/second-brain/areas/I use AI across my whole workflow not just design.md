AI shows up all over my day, not just in design. This is the practical side of my belief that [[AI amplifies your process it does not fix it]].

- Dictation: Wispr Flow is my go-to for brain dumping. It also gives my hands a break from being at a keyboard and mouse for 8 plus hours a day.
- Meeting capture: Granola or Gemini for transcripts.
- Prototyping: Cursor and Claude Code to build out design prototypes really quickly, way faster than I ever could in Figma. That's a shift from [[Figma and Bear are my daily tools|Figma being my only build surface]].
- Organizing: Notion AI to organize decisions, projects, documents, and presentations inside the company.

The through line is that AI takes the mechanical load off so I can spend more time on judgment and [[AI makes taste the differentiator|taste]]. Hubs: [[PKM MOC]], [[Design MOC]].
