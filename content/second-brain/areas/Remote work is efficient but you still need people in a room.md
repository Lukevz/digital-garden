I think remote work is highly efficient when it's done properly, but it does sometimes isolate teams and make it harder to build camaraderie. I work from home and I'm a fan of it, but I don't pretend it's free.

## Virtual meetings and the culture split

What's tough is that Zoom and virtual meetings are draining for a lot of people, especially if you're neurodivergent. There are fewer cues to pick up on, so your brain is doing actual extra work to fill the gaps. This is part of what makes me [[Right now I am most curious about neurodivergence and creativity|curious about neurodivergence]] and team culture.

The thing that helps me is separating meetings into two kinds: culture-building meetings and productivity or workshop-focused meetings. When you know which one you're in, both go better. And nothing beats getting up in person, whether it's brainstorming or just getting to know someone. Doing that somewhat often, once a quarter or a couple of times a year, is healthy.
