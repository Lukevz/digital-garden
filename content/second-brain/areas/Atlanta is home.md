Born and raised in Atlanta, GA, and it's home today. My wife and I lived in Nashville, TN for a few years after we got married, then moved back to Atlanta in 2023.

What most people don't realize about Atlanta is that it's really a collection of cities. It has boroughs the way New York does, each with its own charm, and I love getting to explore them.
