My taste is minimal and clean, generally, but I've always had a soft spot for skeuomorphism. That moment when we went from it to flat design with iOS 7, I think a lot of designers just accepted orders instead of pushing back.

Liquid glass is the most recent thing I genuinely love. The rollout was a separate conversation, but the boldness of trying something completely new deserves respect. That's what I've always admired about Apple. Skeuomorphism is also a case of [[The best patterns come from the physical world not other apps]] done literally. More taste context at [[Design MOC]].
