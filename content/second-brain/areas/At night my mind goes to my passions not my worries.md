At night is when I feel the most clarity, and where my mind goes is usually my passions, not my worries. It's quiet, the world slows down, and there are no meetings or distractions pulling at me. As [[I am a night owl who gets random morning bursts|a night owl]], that late quiet is when my head actually opens up.

Unless I'm in a genuinely stressful situation at work or a particularly stressful season, my brain doesn't default to anxiety at night. It's not always stressful. A lot of the time it's a problem that's been wearing away at my brain, something I'm stuck on, and the quiet is when it finally cracks open. I get these crazy moments of clarity and I'll spin up a note and brain dump, or even set up some agents to run overnight on it.

The rest of the time it just drifts to the stuff I love most: watching Star Wars, researching camera gear, reading about space, or digging into AI and technology. So the honest answer to what keeps me up at night is usually curiosity or a problem I can't put down, not dread. The hobbies behind that are all over [[Life MOC]], and the work version of my mind running is [[I am wired for pattern recognition]].
