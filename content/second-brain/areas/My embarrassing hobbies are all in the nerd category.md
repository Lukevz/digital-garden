I have a couple of hobbies I'm "embarrassed" about, and they all fall in the nerd category: mechanical keyboards, general desk setups and accessories, and note-taking, specifically digital note-taking apps and systems. I really love them. The embarrassment is mostly a joke, because these are the things I'll happily lose an evening to.

The keyboard and desk one shows up in what I actually own, see [[My desk is a dual 5K BenQ setup]], where the mechanical keyboard is a NuPhy Halo75 V2. The note-taking one is less a hobby than the thing my whole public output is built on: [[Figma and Bear are my daily tools]], [[File over app is why I keep everything in markdown]], and [[Things 3 is the todo app I settled on]] are all downstream of it, and I liked the problem enough to start building my own app, [[52 is about owning your thinking again|52]]. I've also written up the todo app search at /#writing/the-search-for-the-best-todo-app.

There's a real pattern here, which is that all three are about the setup rather than the output: the keyboard, the desk, and the system you capture into. That's probably the same wiring as [[I am wired for pattern recognition]], pointed at my own environment. Hubs: [[Life MOC]], [[PKM MOC]].
