It varies by day, but since I work from home, most days look like this: get up, make coffee, have breakfast, and ease into things with a slow start. Some stretches I'll add a voice journal to recap the day before and get my thoughts out, though that comes and goes; [[Journaling is a habit I cannot make stick|journaling has never really stuck for me]]. Email is the first thing I check in the morning. Then I work pretty solidly from 9 to 5, hit the gym in the evening right after work or go on a walk, and keep evenings low-key: tinkering with code, watching TV, reading, or hanging out with friends and family. I like working from [[I like working from Atlanta coffee shops|coffee shops]] too.

## Weekends are for detaching

Weekends I try to detach from work as hard as I can. Ideally I don't open my laptop at all. I lean into hobbies like [[Photography scratches the same itch as design|photography]], video games, or just listening to music, go on a hike, or do something physical. Getting outside and out of my office is how I reset. What an ideal day looks like is at [[A good day is deep work then something out in the world]]. Hub: [[Life MOC]].
