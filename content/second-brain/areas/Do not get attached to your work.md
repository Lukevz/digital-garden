Don't get attached to work. We're generally responsible for delivering something for someone else. It's okay to throw it all away and start over. It's also okay to produce something you're proud of and get very critical feedback on it. Both of those are just part of the job.

This is one of the two things I wish more designers understood, alongside [[Think like a business owner not an order-taker]]. More at [[Design MOC]].
