There's a trend in tech right now to build the tools you need yourself instead of paying for them, and I think it could go either way. Some products really are mostly marketing and honestly aren't hard to build, so you could do it yourself.

## But joy and craft are worth paying for

Even so, I think there's real value in investing in well-built tools. Some software just brings me joy and I rely on it every day, things like MyMind, Granola, and Bear. It can look simple, but building a genuinely good product is not as easy as everyone thinks, even with AI in the mix. There's also a certain trust you get to have with a tool that's well made. If a piece of software brings you joy, it's a pleasure to use, and I don't think you should discount that. This is the flip side of my belief that [[AI amplifies your process it does not fix it]] and that [[Simple is the most elegant form of complexity|simple is the hardest thing to build]].
