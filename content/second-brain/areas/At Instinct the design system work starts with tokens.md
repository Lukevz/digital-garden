At Instinct, the veterinary software company where I design, the internal design system is called Axon and I manage it. We're in the early stages of full design system work, and I'm approaching it tokens-first. After everything I learned at PwC and before, that's the order of operations I trust: get the token layer right and the rest of the system has something solid to stand on, without sacrificing quality or performance along the way.

It's a different problem than PwC. There I had a custom system for thousands of products and complete control; here the job is applying [[Design tokens are the unlock|the tokens lesson]] at a smaller company's scale, where [[Most companies do not need a custom design system|custom-versus-base]] is a live question rather than a settled one.
