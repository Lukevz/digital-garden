My general approach to design is that design isn't really one discipline. In a lot of ways it's taking the best of several others and combining them. You take on an engineering mindset, you take on a product manager mindset, and at the same time you're trying to interpret your customers' and users' pain points. The job is finding the perfect Venn diagram between all of those.

That overlap is not something you develop a skill or a muscle for once and then coast on. You have to hone in on it for every single project, because the right balance of engineering, product, and user reality shifts each time. This is the same instinct behind starting with the [[Six questions before touching any tools]] and choosing to [[Think like a business owner not an order-taker]]. Honestly, "approach to design" is a broad question, and I can give you something more useful if you get specific about the kind of design. The fuller picture of how I think lives at [[Design MOC]].
