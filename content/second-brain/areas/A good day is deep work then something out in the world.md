A good work day is a solid deep work session, ideally with another creative or two. A small, focused creative workshop is where I come alive, especially whiteboarding with a colleague and really getting into the nitty-gritty. Go out for lunch, take a real break, then come back and tackle it again.

A good personal day is a slow morning, sleeping in, going out with my wife for coffee, maybe shopping, then some kind of adventure: concert, movie, hike, something out in nature. Just being out and doing something. The everyday version of this is [[My daily routine runs 9 to 5 from home]]. Hub: [[Life MOC]].
