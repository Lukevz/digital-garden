Journaling is the habit I've tried hardest to build and kept failing at. I've tried a physical journal, a visual one with photos or drawings, and even an icon-based one where you just pick how you're feeling instead of writing anything. I got into voice journaling for a couple of months, which is the version referenced in [[My daily routine runs 9 to 5 from home]].

The problem was that it often didn't actually help me. It started to feel like I was keeping a log, or doing it just to maintain the streak, even when I didn't feel anything or get anything out of it. So it came and went rather than sticking as a daily thing.

What I eventually worked out is that the failure was in what I was asking of it, not in the habit: I kept trying to make every entry future-proof, aesthetic, or useful later. Dropping that is what fixed it, and the version that stuck is at [[Morning pages finally made journaling stick]]. Hub: [[Life MOC]].
