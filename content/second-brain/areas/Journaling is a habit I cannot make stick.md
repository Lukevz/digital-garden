Journaling is the habit I've tried hardest to build and keep failing at. I've tried a physical journal, a visual one with photos or drawings, and even an icon-based one where you just pick how you're feeling instead of writing anything. I got into voice journaling for a couple of months this year, which is the version referenced in [[My daily routine runs 9 to 5 from home]].

The problem is that it often doesn't actually help me. It starts to feel like I'm keeping a log, or doing it just to maintain the streak, even when I don't feel anything or get anything out of it. So it comes and goes rather than sticking as a daily thing. Hub: [[Life MOC]].
