It varies by day, but since I work from home, most days look like this: get up, make coffee, have breakfast, and do a voice journal to get my thoughts out. I recap the day before and process whatever's on my mind before I start. Then I work pretty solidly from 9 to 5, head to the gym or go on a walk, and keep evenings low-key: tinkering with code, watching TV, reading, or hanging out with friends and family.

## Weekends are for detaching

Weekends I try to detach from work as hard as I can. Ideally I don't open my laptop at all. I lean into hobbies like [[Photography scratches the same itch as design|photography]], video games, or just listening to music, go on a hike, or do something physical. Getting outside and out of my office is how I reset. What an ideal day looks like is at [[A good day is deep work then something out in the world]]. Hub: [[Life MOC]].
