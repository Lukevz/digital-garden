The underappreciated thing about AI is what it does to the value of taste. When tools can execute for almost anyone, the differentiator becomes judgment: having an eye that's been trained, a sense of what's actually good versus what's just plausible.

You have to be self-aware and attuned to the world, to real art, to keep honing that. It's a human process. You should never outsource your thinking. That belief runs through everything from [[AI amplifies your process it does not fix it]] to why I'm building [[52 is about owning your thinking again|52]]. More at [[Design MOC]].
