Designers need to think like business owners, not order-takers. Ask how someone got to the problem, not just how to solve the one they handed you.

It's the thing my 22-year-old self needed to hear: ask more questions about how people arrived at the problem, not just about the problem itself. It's easy to go hard on solving what someone puts in front of you. It's harder, and more valuable, to understand how they got there. It pairs with [[Do not get attached to your work]], and the [[Six questions before touching any tools]] are how I put it into practice. More at [[Design MOC]].
