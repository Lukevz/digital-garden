Photography is my big hobby. It's as creatively fulfilling as design but without the tech-problem-solving layer, and it gets me outside and away from screens, which is exactly what I need after a week at a desk. I love the gear side of it too.

My camera roll is mostly nature photos, but I'm actively trying to branch out into street photography, taking pictures of people, and food. The gear side runs deep too, and camera and gear videos are a YouTube rabbit hole I fall into often. Videography rides along with it, and hiking and travel mostly happen in service of it. What I shoot with is at [[I shoot Fujifilm]], and how it fits my week is at [[My daily routine runs 9 to 5 from home]].

What I've actually shot is up on my site at /#photos, with the camera, lens, and settings shown next to each photo; see [[Site MOC]]. The practice side of it is photo walks, one of the habits in [[In 2026 I am building habits that regulate instead of optimize]]. Hub: [[Photography MOC]].
