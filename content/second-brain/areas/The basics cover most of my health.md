The basic stuff covers most of my health. Eating well, sleeping well, and working out enough handles maybe 70 percent of it, and that includes mental health. Sleeping roughly the same amount every night and getting enough movement through the day both have a huge effect. The [[The Oura Ring is the piece of tech I love most|Oura Ring]] is still my go-to for keeping an eye on it, a year and a half in.

## Staying in motion at a desk

The hard part is that I work at a desk all day, every day. A walking treadmill and a sit-stand desk have really helped me stay moving, even at a slow pace, while I'm workshopping ideas or dictating and taking notes. It's the same instinct as the gym-and-walks half of [[My daily routine runs 9 to 5 from home|my daily routine]]: keep the body moving so the head stays clear. Hub: [[Life MOC]].
