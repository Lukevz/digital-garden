Document the points, not the maneuvers. I got this from watching Formula 1: the standings only record points, 25 for a win, 18 for second, and that clean tally is what survives the season. But the race itself is made of maneuvers, the overtakes, the pit calls, the tire management, the near misses. Those are what made it worth watching, and none of them show up in the table.

Gardening ideas works the same way. The maneuvers are the raw ingredients of my thinking: the articles I read, the notes I scribble, the conversations I'm reacting to. The points are the distilled insights I decide to keep, the published note, the idea worth carrying forward. You can try to capture every maneuver and you'll drown in it, and it still won't transmit the energy of being in the race. So I keep the points, credit the ingredients, and let the rest go.

This is the main reason my garden isn't a complete record of my thinking and I'm fine with that. It fits how I see the whole practice in [[A digital garden is a philosophy not a website]], and it's why capture in [[Figma and Bear are my daily tools|Bear]] stays cheap and messy while what reaches the site is deliberate.
