Every year my wife and I pick a two-word theme and then review the year against it. The theme isn't a resolution, it's a lens: it decides which tradeoffs we say yes to for twelve months. 2024 was Consistency and Intentionality. 2025 was Remaking Possible. The review at the end is written publicly, which is the part that keeps me honest.

## How the review is structured

I review across the areas of a wheel of life rather than one big narrative: relationships, work, health, creativity, dwelling, travel, faith, and finance. Going area by area stops the loud parts of a year from swallowing the quiet ones. It's also where I notice the small habits that actually did the work, things like weekly check-ins and biweekly date nights, which look trivial written down and are the reason a hard year didn't break anything.

The theme is what makes it useful a year later. 2024's Consistency and Intentionality was about building the habits. 2025's Remaking Possible was about admitting some things weren't working and rebuilding them: I left PwC for Instinct, see [[PwC was an academy but I wanted to see my work land]] and [[I design software for veterinarians at Instinct]], and we downsized from a four-bedroom house to a two-bedroom apartment. The lesson I carried out of it is that your nervous system matters more than your productivity, which is exactly what 2026 is built on in [[In 2026 I am building habits that regulate instead of optimize]].
