The library is the easy part of a design system rollout. The real job is teaching. When PwC moved to Figma, the growing pains weren't about components: people weren't used to Figma at all, and the way you consumed styles and components was completely different from Sketch. As more teams and designers joined, we needed standards not just for using the design system, but for using Figma itself.

So I started Figma office hours and wrote over 40 pages of documentation: what to look out for if you're coming from Sketch, naming conventions, how to share files, permissions, how to request plugin approvals. All of it stemmed from the design system rollout, but almost none of it was about components.

This is the lived version of [[Design systems are a shared language not a component library]]: a shared language has to be taught, and the teaching is the work. The rebuild that made it all necessary is at [[I rebuilt PwC's design system in Figma to prove the switch]]. Hub: [[Design Systems MOC]].
