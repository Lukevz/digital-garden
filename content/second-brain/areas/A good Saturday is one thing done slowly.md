My favorite way to spend a Saturday is to do one thing, slowly. That might be being by the pool, going on a walk, trying somewhere new to eat, or getting into the city to take photos. I don't try to pack a lot in. I like getting out of the house and doing that single thing at an unhurried pace.

It's the weekend version of [[A good day is deep work then something out in the world]], and it usually involves [[Photography scratches the same itch as design|photography]]. Hub: [[Life MOC]].
