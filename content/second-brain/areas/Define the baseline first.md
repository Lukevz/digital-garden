The first thing I do on any problem is define the baseline: what are people doing today? If users are working in a spreadsheet, I know what that experience feels like, and I know I should never deliver something worse than that.

Defining the baseline tells you what "better" actually means. If you skip it, you have no way to measure whether you succeeded. Most projects skip it and pay for it later. It pairs with the [[Six questions before touching any tools]], which I run before opening any design tool. Both live in my broader take at [[Design MOC]].
