A digital garden is more of a philosophy than a website. It's a mindset for how you approach work, in whatever craft you're in. It lowers the barrier to sharing something publicly while also holding you accountable to closing threads. As a neurodivergent, it's really helpful to let your brain go wherever it wants and follow what catches your eye. The danger is falling into paralysis: bookmarking, reading, consuming, and never creating. The garden fights that.

## Thought-based, not project-based

What makes it different from a blog or YouTube is that it's thought-based, not project-based. Thoughts are ephemeral. They bleed into each other. You can make connections across everything. There's no polished package to ship. You just put it out there and let it grow.
