Simple doesn't mean basic. Simple is the most elegant complexity: distilling something without sacrificing utility or value. That's the bar.

Getting there means iterating past the first workable answer, which is why I lean so hard on the [[Six questions before touching any tools]] up front. You can't distill a problem you haven't defined. Part of my core take at [[Design MOC]].
