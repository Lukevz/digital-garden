I'm a senior product designer at Instinct Science (instinct.vet), where I design software for veterinarians. I joined at the end of 2025 after leaving PwC, the consulting firm where I spent the previous few years.

Veterinarians are overworked, understaffed, under-resourced, and stressed. If I can play a part in reducing that stress and making their lives a little happier, that drives a lot of my design decisions and a lot of my passion for being at Instinct. The full story of the move is at [[PwC was an academy but I wanted to see my work land]], and it's a working example of [[Remove layers between you and the user]]. Hub: [[Career MOC]].
