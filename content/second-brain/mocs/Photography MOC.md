Photography is the hobby that matters most to me because [[Photography scratches the same itch as design]]: same creative fulfillment, none of the tech-problem-solving layer, and it gets me outside. I love the gear side too, and [[I shoot Fujifilm]]: an X-S20 with the Viltrox Pro 27mm f1.2 as the main kit, and an X-M5 with the 27mm f2.8 pancake for daily carry.

The work itself is published at /#photos with the settings shown beside each shot; see [[Site MOC]]. Getting better at it is deliberate now: photo walks are one of the seven habits in [[In 2026 I am building habits that regulate instead of optimize]], with a goal of at least ten this year.

Travel and photography feed each other. The photos from [[South Africa gave me one of the best trips of my life|the South Africa trip]] are some I cherish most, and [[Japan is next on my travel list|Japan]] is high on the list partly for the shooting. The rest of life around this is at [[Life MOC]].
