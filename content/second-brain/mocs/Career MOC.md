I've been designing for about 12 to 13 years, and the whole arc is in [[My career runs from YouTube tutorials to Instinct]]: a self-started YouTube channel, freelance and my own creative production company, client work at Blue Corona, then PwC, then Instinct. The PwC years are where I went deep professionally: [[I pitched and led PwC's migration to Figma]], [[I ran the design system serving 3000 products at PwC]], and [[SDLC Canvas was PwC's flagship AI product]].

I left because [[PwC was an academy but I wanted to see my work land]], and now [[I design software for veterinarians at Instinct]], which is the closest my work has ever been to the people it helps. On the side, [[I run two YouTube channels]] and I'm building [[52 is about owning your thinking again|52]], though I'm [[Not taking freelance work right now|not taking freelance work]]. The mindset I'd pass to younger designers is [[Think like a business owner not an order-taker]]. The move from PwC to Instinct and what it cost is written up honestly in [[I do an annual review built on a yearly theme]].
