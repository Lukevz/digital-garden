Design systems are my specialty, and my core take is that [[Design systems are a shared language not a component library]]. The components are the output; the real work is the decisions, the documentation, and adoption. I like design systems because they force you to think at the system level, which is where my head already lives; see [[I am wired for pattern recognition]].

Most of my track record here is from PwC, the consulting firm where I spent 2022 through 2025: [[I ran the design system serving 3000 products at PwC]] as manager, and before that [[I pitched and led PwC's migration to Figma]], which meant rebuilding the system to prove the tool and then teaching 120+ designers. Today the systems thinking continues at Instinct, where [[I design software for veterinarians at Instinct]]. The broader philosophy that systems work sits inside is at [[Design MOC]], and the career arc is at [[Career MOC]].
