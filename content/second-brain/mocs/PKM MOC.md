My whole approach to personal knowledge management starts from the belief that [[A digital garden is a philosophy not a website]]. It's thought-based instead of project-based, it lowers the barrier to sharing, and as a neurodivergent it lets my brain follow what catches its eye without falling into consume-only paralysis. The public face of that is [[lukevz.com is hand-built with vanilla HTML CSS and JS|lukevz.com]], and the private capture layer is Bear; see [[Figma and Bear are my daily tools]].

The newest thread is [[52 is about owning your thinking again|52]], my local markdown note-taking app, which comes from the same conviction as [[AI makes taste the differentiator]]: never outsource your thinking. The books that shaped how I think about creative work and trust are at [[Creativity Inc and The Speed of Trust are my two book recommendations]]. The daily practice that feeds all of this, including the morning voice journal, is at [[My daily routine runs 9 to 5 from home]].
