My site, lukevz.com, is a digital garden split into browsable sections, so when something I've made covers a question I can point straight at it instead of summarizing it badly. How the site is built is at [[lukevz.com is hand-built with vanilla HTML CSS and JS]], and why it exists at all is at [[A digital garden is a philosophy not a website]]. This note is the map of what lives where, and I keep it current as the site grows.

The four sections in the top bar are Career, Writing, Videos, and Photos. Career is the home view. Everything else hangs off the launchpad grid or the overflow menu.

## Photos

My photography is at /#photos. Clicking a photo opens it full size with the camera, lens, focal length, aperture, shutter, and ISO beside it, because I care about that stuff and I assume anyone who clicks does too. Most of it is shot on Fujifilm, see [[I shoot Fujifilm]], and why I shoot at all is at [[Photography scratches the same itch as design]]. Recent phone shots come in from Instagram automatically; the Fujifilm files keep their full EXIF.
