Things 3 is what I use for personal tasks, and it's been my daily driver for years now. It wins on the three things I actually care about in [[A todo app should be easy to capture unopinionated and clear your mind]]: capture is instant, it doesn't impose a methodology on me, and it gets the list out of my head.

It also helps that it's a beautifully made Mac and iOS app, which matters to me more than it probably should, see [[I am 100 percent Mac and all-in on Apple]] and [[Well-built software that brings joy is worth paying for]]. Team and collaborative work doesn't live here; that goes wherever the team already is, Asana, Notion, Trello, or Todoist.

My full setup and the reasoning behind it is written up at /#writing/the-search-for-the-best-todo-app. Notes and thinking live elsewhere, in Bear, see [[Figma and Bear are my daily tools]]. Hubs: [[PKM MOC]], [[Site MOC]].
