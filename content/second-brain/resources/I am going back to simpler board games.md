I do like board games, but lately I'm coming back to the older, simpler ones. Board games have gotten very complicated, and sometimes I don't want to do all that mental work just to have fun. It's the same taste for simplicity I bring to design, where [[Simple is the most elegant form of complexity]]. For co-op specifically, [[Pode is the co-op game my wife and I keep coming back to]].
