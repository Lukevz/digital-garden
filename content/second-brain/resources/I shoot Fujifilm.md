I shoot Fujifilm. My main body right now is the Fujifilm X-S20, usually paired with the Viltrox Pro 27mm f1.2, my go-to fast prime. I recently picked up the Fujifilm X-M5 as well, and for daily carry I've got the Fujifilm 27mm f2.8 pancake on it. The pancake keeps the whole thing tiny and pocketable, which is exactly what I want when I'm just out and about.
