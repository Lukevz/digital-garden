If I could only hand someone two books: *Creativity, Inc.* and *The Speed of Trust*. *Creativity, Inc.* is about Pixar but it's really about how to build a culture where good work can happen; it's basically Ed Catmull's memoir and I consider it required reading. *The Speed of Trust* changed how I think about relationships, teams, and decisions.

I keep a full bookshelf at lukevz.com/bookshelf. Ed Catmull also shows up in [[Designers and thinkers I come back to]]. Hub: [[PKM MOC]].
