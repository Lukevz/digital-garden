I've been running an espresso machine at home for the last couple of years and it's still going strong for us. I once borrowed a fancier one from a buddy, but it was a lot of work. I admire people who can pull a great cup consistently, I just value the consistency of a reliably good cup over the challenge of chasing a perfect one every day.

That's the same instinct I bring to design: reliable and repeatable beats heroic and inconsistent. On the go I'm currently [[I am on a cold brew kick|on a cold brew kick]] and I have my [[Atlanta coffee shops I recommend|Atlanta coffee shops]]. Hub: [[Life MOC]].
