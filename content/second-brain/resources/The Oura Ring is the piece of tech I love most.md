The Oura Ring is the piece of tech I love most. It does basically everything I need for health and fitness tracking without me having to think about it. If I could only recommend one device to someone, that's the one.

Part of why I chose Oura over something like a smartwatch is that I don't love wearing things on my wrist, jewelry in general really. When I'm going out or dressing up a bit, though, I do have a small analog watch collection I like to wear.

I've compared it to the Apple Watch on [[I run two YouTube channels|my personal YouTube channel]], and it fits the gym-and-walks side of [[My daily routine runs 9 to 5 from home|my routine]]. Hub: [[Life MOC]].
