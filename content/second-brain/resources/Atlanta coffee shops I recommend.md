I live in Atlanta and I'm a bit of a coffee-shop person. The ones I recommend: Stello Kin, Laylo, The Reading Room, Brash, and Spiller Park.

My order is almost always a simple latte. That's deliberate: it's how I test a new coffee shop, because it's simple and easy to compare across the board. Same instinct as [[I run an espresso machine and value consistency over craft|valuing consistency over craft]] at home, and [[I am on a cold brew kick|the cold brew kick]] is the current exception.

Coffee runs with my wife are a staple of [[A good day is deep work then something out in the world|a good day]], and the city context is at [[Atlanta is home]]. Hub: [[Life MOC]].
