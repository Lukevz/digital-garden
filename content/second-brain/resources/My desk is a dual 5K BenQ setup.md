I work from home, so the desk matters. Right now I'm running a dual-monitor setup of BenQ MA270S 5K displays, driven by a 14-inch MacBook Pro (M4 Pro). For input I bounce between a Logitech MX Master 4 and the Apple Magic Trackpad. My keyboard is a NuPhy Halo75 V2 mechanical, and I record on a Shure MV7 mic.

It's not all tech. I keep a few 3D-printed toys on the desk, a mini Baymax bobblehead, a Toothless, a Light Fury, and the cat from KPop Demon Hunters, plus some fidgets like a spinner and little magnetic clips that go back and forth. They give my hands something to do while I think.

The mic earns its keep for [[I run two YouTube channels|YouTube]], and the whole setup exists in service of [[My daily routine runs 9 to 5 from home|a work-from-home routine]]. Hub: [[Life MOC]].
