Designers I admire: Dan Petty, Tobias van Schneider, Tobias Renstrom, and I'm a huge fan of Ryo Lu.

Creators and thinkers, including the YouTubers I actually watch: Ali Abdaal for the productivity mindset, and Olivier from Ultra Links. Those two are my answer when someone asks who I follow or what channels I keep up with. Then the classic answers: Steve Jobs, Bob Iger, Ed Catmull. Catmull's [[Creativity Inc and The Speed of Trust are my two book recommendations|Creativity, Inc.]] is required reading.
