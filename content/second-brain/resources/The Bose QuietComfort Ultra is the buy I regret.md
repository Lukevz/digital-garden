For headphones I run Bose QuietComfort Ultra and AirPods Pro, and honest take: the Bose are the one buy I sort of regret. I genuinely love them for travel, but they don't stay on my head well, so I barely use them for workouts, and for focused work they feel almost too isolating. Heads-down, I actually prefer wearing nothing at all.

They're a great travel headphone. I just don't travel enough to justify owning those and the AirPods. What I actually listen to while working is at [[Lo-fi and Brain FM are my work soundtrack]], and the rest of my kit is at [[My desk is a dual 5K BenQ setup]]. Hub: [[Life MOC]].
