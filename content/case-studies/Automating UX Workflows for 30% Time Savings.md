Pioneering the integration of Generative AI into UX processes, I led an initiative to develop custom AI-driven tools that automated repetitive tasks, enhanced productivity, and fostered creativity across design teams. These GPTs transformed workflows, improved quality, and freed up time for designers and managers to focus on high-value creative work.

## The Challenge

The design team faced an ongoing challenge of repetitive, routine tasks that consumed a significant portion of their time. Managers and designers were frequently bogged down with processes such as writing release notes, pairing brand assets, and drafting content — which hindered the team's ability to focus on higher-level creative work.

## My Approach

**Identifying Opportunities for Automation**

I started by identifying repetitive and time-consuming tasks that were common across design workflows. After consulting with both managers and individual contributors, I focused on creating custom AI-driven tools tailored to specific pain points.

**Developing the GPT Suite**

**UX Copywriting Assistant**

Trained on Nielsen Norman Group articles and UX writing best practices, this tool helped designers generate microcopy for titles, subtitles, buttons, and labels. Designers could provide context or paste wireframe screenshots, and the tool would suggest copy, speeding up the ideation phase.

![UX Copywriting Assistant — generates on-brand microcopy from context or wireframe screenshots](/images/case-studies/Copywriter.png)

**Brand Icon Pairing Tool**

Trained on our design system's icon library, this tool allowed designers to input copy and receive three relevant icon suggestions, ensuring consistent brand representation without manual hunting through the library.

![Brand Icon Pairing Tool — suggests three on-brand icons from any input copy](/images/case-studies/Icon pair.png)

**Performance Reviewer**

Developed to assist in performance reviews, this tool used predefined criteria to transform conversational input into structured feedback adhering to character limits and specific parameters — a task that had previously taken managers significant time to format manually.

**Task Builder**

Created to streamline task delegation, this tool transformed simple prompts or voice memos into well-structured tasks categorized by Summary, Context, Instructions, and References.

![Task Builder — turns voice memos and rough notes into structured, actionable tasks](/images/case-studies/Task builder.png)

**Release Notes Generator**

To improve clarity for end users, this tool transformed technical changelog notes into concise, user-friendly release notes categorized into Overview, Features, Enhancements, and Bugs Fixed.

![Release Notes Generator — converts technical changelogs into clear, user-friendly notes](/images/case-studies/Release notes.png)

## Impact

- **Tangible Results:** Managers reported gaining up to 30% more time each week, allowing them to focus on strategic leadership. Routine documents such as release notes were produced significantly faster, reducing turnaround times.
- **Intangible Results:** Team morale improved as tedious tasks were alleviated, fostering a culture of creativity and innovation. Designers had more bandwidth to invest in exploration and refinement, resulting in better design outcomes and a more engaged team overall.

## Reflections

Incorporating Generative AI into UX workflows showcased the potential of AI to significantly reduce the burden of repetitive tasks. However, aligning the tools to team needs required constant iteration and user feedback.

If I'd do one thing differently: I would initiate more frequent user feedback sessions earlier in the project to better understand how each tool fit into the team's actual workflow and make adjustments sooner. The tools that got the most adoption were the ones shaped most by the people using them.
