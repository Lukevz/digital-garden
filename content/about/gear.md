## Cameras and lenses

I shoot Fujifilm. My main body right now is the Fujifilm X-S20, usually paired with the Viltrox Pro 27mm f1.2 — that's my go-to fast prime. I recently picked up the Fujifilm X-M5 as well, and for daily carry I've got the Fujifilm 27mm f2.8 pancake on it. The pancake keeps the whole thing tiny and pocketable, which is exactly what I want when I'm just out and about.

## Wearables and everyday tech

The Oura Ring is the piece of tech I love most. It does basically everything I need for health and fitness tracking without me having to think about it. If I could only recommend one device to someone, that's the one.

For headphones I run Bose QuietComfort Ultra and AirPods Pro. Honest take: the Bose are the one buy I sort of regret. I genuinely love them for travel, but they don't stay on my head well, so I barely use them for workouts, and for focused work they feel almost too isolating — heads-down, I actually prefer wearing nothing at all. They're a great travel headphone, I just don't travel enough to justify owning those *and* the AirPods.

## Desk and WFH setup

I work from home, so the desk matters. Right now I'm running a dual-monitor setup of BenQ MA270S 5K displays, driven by a 14-inch MacBook Pro (M4 Pro). For input I bounce between a Logitech MX Master 4 and the Apple Magic Trackpad. My keyboard is a NuPhy Halo75 V2 mechanical, and I record on a Shure MV7 mic.
