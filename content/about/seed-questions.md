# Seed questions to bootstrap your knowledge base

<!--

## About me (→ bio.md, personal.md)

1. What's the one-sentence pitch for who you are?
2. Where did you grow up, and how does it show up in how you work?
3. What's your earliest memory of caring about design?
4. What was your first real job? What did it teach you?
5. What do you do on a Saturday with no plans?
6. What's a hobby you're embarrassed to admit you love?
7. What music is on heavy rotation right now?
8. What three things would you take to a desert island?
9. What's the weirdest thing about you most people don't know?
10. What do your friends tease you about?

## Work (→ work.md)

11. What does a typical workday look like?
12. What part of your job energizes you most?
13. What part drains you?
14. What's a project you're proud of, and why?
15. What's a project that went badly, and what did you learn?
16. How do you handle disagreement with stakeholders?
17. What's your default tool stack right now?
18. What's a tool you've outgrown?
19. How do you decide what to work on next?
20. What would you tell a junior designer just joining your team?

## Philosophy (→ philosophy.md)

21. What's the difference between good and great design to you?
22. What's a design opinion you'd defend that others push back on?
23. Who are 3 people who shaped how you think? Why each?
24. What's your relationship with productivity systems?
25. How do you stay creative without burning out?
26. What does "taste" mean to you, and how did you build yours?
27. When do you trust your gut vs. data?
28. What's a recurring frame or heuristic you find yourself using?
29. What's something you used to believe about design that you now disagree with?
30. How do you handle creative blocks?

## Voice (→ voice-and-tone.md)

31. Paste 3 DMs or short messages you sent recently.
32. Paste the opening paragraph of 3 of your favorite posts you've written.
33. What's a phrase you say all the time?
34. What's a word you avoid because it feels lazy?
35. How do you start an email to someone you don't know yet?
36. How do you tell someone bad news in a message?
37. How do you say "I disagree" without being a jerk?
38. What's your default sign-off?
39. How would your closest friend describe your humor?
40. Pick 3 of your posts. What's one sentence from each that feels MOST like you?

## Now (→ now.md)

41. What are you actively building or shipping?
42. What are you reading?
43. What are you avoiding?
44. What's a question you're sitting with?
45. What did you change your mind about in the last 6 months?

## FAQ (→ faq.md)

46. What are the 3 questions strangers always ask you at events?
47. What's the question on Twitter/X that draws the most replies?
48. What do friends ask for your advice on?
49. What do junior designers DM you about?
50. What's a question you wish more people would ask you?
