## Disney

I'm a big Disney fan. I love Disney World and going to all the theme parks — it's one of my favorite kinds of trips, and I'll happily talk parks with anyone.

## South Africa

My family is South African, and my last trip there was over 13 years ago now, but it's still one of the best trips of my life. We rode elephants, went on safaris, and did a hot air balloon — genuinely once-in-a-lifetime stuff. I cherish the photos from that visit, and I'd love to find a way back to do it all again.
