## Family situation (high level)

Married. Two cats — Lena and Lily.

## Where I'm from / where I live

Born and raised in Atlanta, GA. My family is from South Africa and moved to Atlanta in 1994 — I was the only one born here. My wife and I lived in Nashville, TN for a few years after we got married, then moved back to Atlanta in 2023.

## Hobbies

- Photography and videography — as creatively fulfilling as design but gets me outside and away from screens; love the gear side of it too.
- Working out and long walks.
- F1 and traveling.
- Video games.
- Tinkering with code on side projects.

## What I do for fun

Photography is the big one — it scratches the same creative itch as design without the tech-problem-solving layer. I love that it gets me out of the house. Otherwise I'm watching F1, going on walks, or planning the next trip somewhere.

## What a good day looks like

Work: a solid deep work session, ideally with another creative or two. A small, focused creative workshop is where I come alive — especially whiteboarding with a colleague and really getting into the nitty-gritty. Go out for lunch, take a real break, then come back and tackle it again.

Personal: slow morning, sleep in, go out with my wife for coffee, maybe shopping, then some kind of adventure — concert, movie, hike, something out in nature. Just being out and doing something.

## Music I like

Mostly lo-fi when I'm working — Lo-Fi Girl is on a lot. I also run Brain FM in the background because I want something ambient with no lyrics that fades out while I focus. Not really a genre person — it's more about whether it gets out of the way.

## My daily routine

It varies by day, but since I work from home most days look like this: get up, make coffee, have breakfast, and do a voice journal to get my thoughts out — recap the day before and process whatever's on my mind before I start. I work pretty solidly from 9 to 5, then head to the gym or go on a walk. Evenings are low-key: tinkering with code, watching TV, reading, or hanging out with friends and family.

Weekends I try to detach from work as hard as I can. Ideally I don't open my laptop at all. I lean into hobbies — photography, video games, just listening to music — go on a hike, or do something physical. Getting outside and out of my office is how I reset.

## Do I play any instruments

Yeah — drums are my main one, and I can play a little bit of keys. I haven't played much lately, but it's still something I love and want to get back into.

## Sports / outdoor stuff

F1 — big fan. Follow the season closely. Hiking and being outside shows up more as part of photography and travel than a dedicated sport.

## Heritage

South African on my family's side — they moved to Atlanta in 1994 and I was the only one born here. I'm proud of that heritage. I still have family in South Africa; haven't been able to visit in some time but have been a few times with family. It's a gorgeous country.
